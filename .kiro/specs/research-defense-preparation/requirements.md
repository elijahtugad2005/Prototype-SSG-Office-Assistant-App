# Requirements: Research Defense Preparation

## Defense Overview

- **Project:** SSG Office Assistant App — A Digital Management System for Student Government Operations
- **Defense Date:** Monday, May 5, 2026
- **Duration:** 60–80 minutes
- **Team:** Bliza, Frances, Raniza, Justin, Elijah, Reyvehn (6 members)
- **Panel:** Research defense committee (institution faculty)

---

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1: Team Role Assignment
- Each team member must have a clearly defined primary role and a backup role
- Role assignments must cover: Lead Presenter, Research Presenters (×2), Technical Presenter, Demo Operator, Q&A Coordinator
- Every chapter (1–7) must be assigned to a specific presenter

### FR-2: Chapter Coverage
- All 7 chapters of the research paper must be presented
- Each chapter must have a designated presenter and estimated time allocation
- Chapter 4 and 5 (Results & Discussion) must include system screenshots and technical explanations

### FR-3: Live System Demonstration
- The live demo must cover all major system modules: Member Management, Product Management, Order Management, Finance/Budget, Inventory, Documents, Announcements, and Admin Panel
- A backup plan (recorded video or screenshots) must be prepared in case of technical failure
- Demo must be tested on the defense laptop at least 24 hours before the defense

### FR-4: Q&A Preparation
- The team must prepare answers for at least 15 anticipated panel questions
- Questions must be categorized: Technical, Research/Methodology, and Conceptual
- Each question must have a designated primary responder

### FR-5: Visual Aids
- A complete slide deck must be prepared covering all chapters, the live demo transition, Q&A, and closing
- Printed copies of the research paper must be available for each panel member plus one spare
- All slides must be proofread and finalized at least 24 hours before the defense

### FR-6: Timeline Management
- A detailed defense timeline must be followed with time allocations per section
- A designated timer (Raniza) must monitor time and signal presenters at the 1-minute warning
- Total presentation time must not exceed 80 minutes

### FR-7: Pre-Defense Preparation
- A full rehearsal must be completed 48 hours before the defense (Saturday, May 3)
- All checklist items for 48 hours, 24 hours, and day-of must be completed
- The team must arrive at the venue 30–45 minutes early on defense day

### FR-8: Contingency Planning
- Contingency responses must be defined for: demo failure, presenter losing track, projector failure, absent team member, and time overrun
- All team members must be aware of contingency plans before the defense

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-1: Professionalism
- All team members must wear formal attire on defense day
- Presentations must be delivered confidently without reading directly from slides
- Transitions between presenters must be smooth and rehearsed

### NFR-2: Completeness
- No chapter may be skipped unless a contingency situation forces it
- The live demo must demonstrate at least 8 of the 10 planned modules

### NFR-3: Time Compliance
- Individual chapter presentations must stay within their allocated time windows
- The Q&A session must be given a minimum of 15 minutes

### NFR-4: Technical Reliability
- The defense laptop must have the system running and tested before the defense
- An HDMI cable and display adapter must be available
- A backup demo video must be recorded and stored locally (not dependent on internet)

### NFR-5: Communication
- All team members must review the Q&A preparation notes before the defense
- The team must conduct at least one full rehearsal together
- Bliza (Lead) must coordinate any last-minute changes to the plan

---

## Acceptance Criteria

| Requirement | Acceptance Condition |
|---|---|
| All chapters presented | Chapters 1–7 each have a presenter and are delivered |
| Live demo functional | At least 8 modules demonstrated without critical failure |
| Q&A handled | All panel questions receive a response from the designated member |
| Time within bounds | Defense completes within 60–80 minutes |
| Printed copies ready | One copy per panel member available before defense starts |
| Rehearsal completed | Full run-through done by Saturday, May 3 |
| Backup plan ready | Demo video or screenshots prepared and accessible |
| Formal attire | All 6 members in formal dress on defense day |
